OPERATION DARK HORIZON

A Space Vehicle Cyber Attack Tabletop Exercise

📋 Mission Briefing

You are the senior operations officer at the ground control center for SENTINEL-7, a commercial Earth-observation satellite used for weather forecasting and disaster response. A sophisticated adversary (code-named Celestial Dragon) has initiated a cyber campaign against your ground segment and spacecraft.

Like the classic Oregon Trail, you will face a series of critical situations and must choose from three options at each turn. Your decisions will determine whether SENTINEL-7 survives — or falls to the adversary.

🎯 Exercise Objectives

  • Understand how realistic cyber attacks on space vehicles unfold
  • Recognize indicators visible from a satellite command center
  • Identify SPARTA & MITRE ATT&CK techniques adversaries use
  • Learn which spacecraft subsystems each technique puts at risk
  • Discover which NIST SP 800-53 controls could prevent or detect each attack

🛰️ Spacecraft Subsystems You're Protecting

📡 Command & Control Ground-to-space uplink/downlink & mission control software
📻 Communications Transponders, encryption modules, & comm protocols
💾 Flight Software Onboard flight computers, attitude control, & firmware
🛰️ Payload Sensors, cameras, & mission-specific instruments

📊 How Scoring Works

Each decision earns Defender Points or hands the adversary Attacker Points. Poor choices also degrade specific spacecraft subsystems. At the end of 5 stages, the outcome reflects your cumulative decisions.

⚠️ No cyber expertise required. This exercise is designed for all personnel — focus on sound judgment, teamwork, and situational awareness.
